NORTHERN TRANSFORMATION INITIATIVE (NTI)
Restoring Dignity. Expanding Opportunity. Delivering Measurable Impact.
Northern Transformation Initiative (NTI) is a registered nonprofit organization committed to strengthening vulnerable communities through education access, maternal support, livelihood development, and structured humanitarian programs.

Founded in 2011, NTI operates with integrity, transparency, and measurable impact across Kenya and the region.

We believe generosity should produce results — not dependency.

GRACE BRIDGE INITIATIVE
Our Current Focus
The Grace Bridge Initiative is NTI’s active humanitarian project supporting vulnerable mothers, children, and underserved families.

The initiative provides:

• Temporary housing assistance for young mothers
• Access to medical care
• Food and nutrition support
• Education sponsorship (books, uniforms, tuition assistance)
• Sanitary dignity programs for schoolgirls
• Skills training and empowerment workshops
• Structured mentorship and reintegration support

Grace Bridge represents structured compassion — practical solutions designed to restore stability and long-term opportunity.

Inspired by Grace Rosado
Grace Bridge Initiative is inspired by Grace Rosado, founder of New Life Home in Manchester, New Hampshire.

Grace Rosado has devoted years of her life to serving vulnerable families with humility, consistency, and integrity. Her leadership reflects disciplined compassion — generosity paired with responsibility and accountability.

Her work reminds us that authentic service is not about recognition — it is about impact.

Grace Bridge carries that same spirit forward.

FOUNDER & EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR
Adan Muktar
Founder & Executive Director

Adan founded Northern Transformation Initiative with a commitment to ethical leadership and structured community development.

As the author of Memoirs of a Lost Boy: A Journey of Identity, Adan shares his lived experience of displacement, resilience, and the transformative power of education.

His leadership is grounded in:

• First-hand understanding of hardship
• Commitment to financial transparency
• Belief in education as a pathway to independence
• Cross-cultural engagement between Africa and the United States

From Boston, MA, Adan oversees U.S. partnerships, fundraising strategy, international collaboration, and program development.

A Voice Born from Experience
“School was my sanctuary. Every day, I walked barefoot over dust and stones to reach a small classroom… Inside, I could dream.”

The stories documented in Adan’s books are not fiction detached from reality. They reflect lived experiences of hardship, displacement, institutional instability, and resilience.
